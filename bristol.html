Real leaks on a real network · Bristol, UK · Water Guardian

On 12 and 13 June 2024 researchers opened four hydrants on a real 63 km network with 40 pressure loggers and let water out at 1, 3 and 5 L/s. We gave Water Guardian's locator the time of each leak and the logger readings, and asked one question: where is it? The code was frozen before the run and ran once. The leak time was given, so this tests locating, not detecting.

What this is: a display of one frozen test (our approved claim 17). Nothing is computed on this page. Limits: four leaks; the eleven stages are not independent; the hydrant positions were fitted from a figure in the authors' thesis and are not yet confirmed by them; the settings were tuned on two other leaks the day before; this is the known-time locator, not the year-round detector. The survey order (nearest pipe first) is a convenience, not a result: on a fresh benchmark test it beat a random order only modestly. Network, loggers and leak trials: Liu, Pecci and Stoianov, Imperial College London, "Field Validation of Hydraulic Model-Based Leak Localization Methods in Water Distribution Networks", Zenodo 16737160, CC BY 4.0.
