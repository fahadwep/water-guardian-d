Real network test: BKTown, Belgium

A real distribution network (5,204 junctions, about 214 km of pipe) with mobile pressure loggers. The utility opened 17 fire hydrants for 20-40 minutes at known times. Guardian's twin (built from the utility's calibrated EPANET model, starting from the model's own simulated state at that moment) was asked where each opening was, using pressure only.

14 / 17clear signal (p < 0.05)
4 / 17located within 300 m
9 / 17located within 1 km
984 mmedian error

What it means: short openings on a real network give weak pressure signals; pressure loggers alone narrow them to about 1 km. This supports Guardian's design: detect with flow, locate with pressure, and confirm in the field.
