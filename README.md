# Water Guardian: demo pages

Static pages for the Water Guardian leak-detection project (IECE 2026 Engineering Hackathon, ChemE Club, Jubail Industrial College).

- `index.html`: overview and results on 10 unseen test years
- `replay.html` / `replay_ar.html`: one year of L-Town 2019, day by day (English / Arabic)
- `ltown_2019.html`: the full-year L-Town 2019 report
- `bktown.html`: real-network test on BKTown, Belgium

Data: BattLeDIM (Vrachimis et al. 2022, Zenodo 4017659, CC BY 4.0); BKTown (Mazaev et al. 2023, Zenodo 7255403, CC BY 4.0).
