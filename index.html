Results

How the numbers were made

The method was developed on development years only, then frozen (code fingerprints recorded), and only then tested once on fresh simulated years it had never seen, scored with the official BattLeDIM rules. The test years come from our copy of the organisers' simulator (it reproduces the official 2019 data to within 3 mm). The new version raises more wrong-place alarms than the previous one (about 28 a year against 8); a lean setting halves that for slightly lower savings.
