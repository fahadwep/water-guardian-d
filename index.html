How the numbers were made

The method was developed on development years only, then frozen (code fingerprints recorded), and only then tested once on fresh simulated years it had never seen, scored with the official BattLeDIM rules. The test years come from our copy of the organisers' simulator (it reproduces the official 2019 data to within 3 mm). Each wrong-place alarm costs one crew visit; the night pressure test is there to catch them before anyone digs.
